# Design decision record

ADR-01: Keep a browser-only modular design. Reason: clear coursework scope, no credentials, free deployment. Trade-off: browser records are not synchronized or access-controlled.
ADR-02: Use deterministic scoring; explain urgency, importance, effort components. Trade-off: a fixed formula reflects assumptions and may not fit all students.
ADR-03: Schedule due-date-first within seven days, never beyond daily capacity. Ranking order may differ from scheduling order.
ADR-04: Make no claim that higher priority scores increase academic performance; compare with deadline-only baseline during evaluation.
